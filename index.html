Large companies have spent billions on systems that know who you are before you speak. That capability used to cost a data warehouse, a CDP, an ML team, and a cookie consent banner. It belonged to companies, not builders.

Presence changes that. One 12KB file. No backend. No cookies. No network calls. Twenty-five browser signals. Nine intent states. Classification in under 2 milliseconds - before your first component renders.

Open DevTools (F12). In the console, type this exactly:

Presence.debug()

Check the Network tab. You will see zero outbound requests. Nothing leaves your browser.

See what was written to localStorage:

localStorage.getItem('_ptv') localStorage.getItem('_psd') localStorage.getItem('_ptop')

Scroll to the bottom of this page, wait 10 seconds, then close and reopen. Your scroll depth and time on page will be stored. Presence will adjust your cognitive mode accordingly on the next visit.

Reset and reload as a fresh visitor:

['_ptv','_pfs','_pls','_psd','_ptop'].forEach(k=>localStorage.removeItem(k));sessionStorage.clear();location.reload();
