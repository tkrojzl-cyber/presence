Large companies have spent billions building systems that figure out who you are before you speak. They call it personalisation. What they mean is: serve the right thing to the right person at the right moment.

That capability used to cost a data warehouse, a CDP, an ML team, and a cookie consent banner. It was locked behind enterprise contracts. It belonged to companies, not builders.

Presence changes that. One 12KB script. No backend. No cookies. No network calls. It reads ten browser signals that were always there, classifies your visitor into one of nine intent states, and tells your page exactly what to show and what to suppress. In under 2 milliseconds. Before your first component renders.

This is what democratising the internet looks like. Not another SaaS dashboard at $500/month. A file. That anyone can drop into any page. And suddenly their site knows the difference between someone who just arrived and someone who is ready to act.

// VERIFY IT YOURSELF

Open DevTools (F12). In the console, type this exactly:

Presence.debug()

Check the Network tab. You will see zero outbound requests. Nothing leaves your browser.

See what was written to localStorage:

localStorage.getItem('_ptv') localStorage.getItem('_psd') localStorage.getItem('_ptop')

Scroll to the bottom of this page, wait 10 seconds, then close and reopen. Your scroll depth and time on page will be stored. Presence will adjust your cognitive mode accordingly on the next visit.

Reset and reload as a fresh visitor:

['_ptv','_pfs','_pls','_psd','_ptop'].forEach(k=>localStorage.removeItem(k));sessionStorage.clear();location.reload();
